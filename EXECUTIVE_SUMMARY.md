## Where each implementation shines

- **indexmap** is the iteration specialist — ~3–4× faster iteration than any hash map at every size — at the price of 2–7× slower lookups and inserts.
- **Persistent maps** (rpds, immutable-chunkmap, imbl) are unique for clone-heavy code: rpds clones a 100k-entry map in ~2 ns (O(1) structural sharing) versus deep-copy µs–ms for the rest, but they are 5–50× slower for inserts and lookups.
